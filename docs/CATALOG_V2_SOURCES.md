# Catalog V2 source feasibility and reuse

Reviewed 23 November 2026. Coverage judgments here describe observed examples and likely gaps. No source was measured against a national census of books. Retrieval rights, public display, storage, and automated text processing need separate decisions.

| Source | Kenyan coverage and metadata | Access and scale | Prototype use and reuse decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open Library | Good starting point for internationally distributed Kenyan books, some older Kenyan editions and Kiswahili titles. Work and edition records expose identifiers, publisher, date, language, ISBN and sometimes subjects, covers or descriptions. The sample found parallel work IDs, extra contributors and edition conflicts. | Public low-volume search, work and edition APIs worked. Open Library asks regular clients to identify themselves and documents about one request/sec unidentified and three/sec identified. Use timestamped monthly dumps for bulk work and edition joins rather than crawling. Covers use a separate service. | Retain selected bibliographic facts and source IDs. Internet Archive disclaims new rights in the database, which does not clear upstream rights in contributor text, covers or scans. Show catalog links. Resolve cover rights and attribution separately. Borrowing and readable previews are not permission to process or store book text. [API](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api) · [dumps](https://openlibrary.org/developers/dumps) · [licensing](https://openlibrary.org/developers/licensing) |
| Google Books | Strong secondary checks for publisher, identifiers and some synopses. Availability can vary by country. The old *Juices* description and *Olduvai Gorge* media came from unrelated volumes. Shared words and an author name in a query are insufficient. | Volumes API, ISBN search, per-volume IDs, quota in the API project. There is no general-purpose Books bulk data export in the documented developer offering. Confirm the quota and access rules for the project before implementing. | Use for independently reviewed lookup only in this phase, and do not call or cache the API from the prototype. Review Books and Google API terms for caching, display and attribution, and commercial use limits. Viewer previews and snippets are not a text-mining licence. [API guide](https://developers.google.com/books/docs/v1/using) · [Volumes](https://developers.google.com/books/docs/v1/reference/volumes) · [Books terms](https://developers.google.com/books/terms) · [Google API terms](https://developers.google.com/terms) |
| Wikidata and Wikibase SPARQL | Useful for author IDs and aliases, citizenship and related works. Incomplete for local presses and poets. Work, edition and similarly named records can coexist. In this sample several work items link to Open Library edition IDs. Language and genre statements can also disagree with publishers. | Action API entity calls worked. SPARQL worked on a small probe. Use bounded queries with explicit entity sets and retain contributor claims and references. Respect documented time, concurrency and throttling limits. Monitor the main/scholarly split and backend migration. Use dated dumps for wider extraction. | Store CC0 structured claims and QIDs. Author citizenship can establish one inclusion basis. Neither linked third-party prose, images nor book text inherits CC0. [Licence](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) · [Query Service](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikidata_Query_Service/User_Manual) · [Data access](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access) |
| Goodreads | Useful for understanding reader-facing language and category design. Tags, ratings, reviews and blurbs have different provenance and are unsuitable as authority records. Kenyan coverage was not measured. | The public API landing page redirected to the main site and then showed a browser challenge in the research client. No operational public onboarding or bulk permission was verified. | No scraping, API calls, copied lists, reviews, ratings or descriptions. Public terms expressly restrict listings reuse and automated extraction. Reassess only with a documented licensed arrangement. [Terms](https://www.goodreads.com/about/terms) · [API entry page](https://www.goodreads.com/api) |
| East African Educational Publishers, Longhorn, Moran and local publishers | High value for Kenyan editions, children’s titles and Kiswahili readers. EAEP has classics and children’s sections, Longhorn presents Kiswahili readers and price lists, and Moran has a catalog PDF. A school list can give real edition details but may not distinguish literary work, study guide and textbook. | EAEP and Longhorn catalog pages responded. Web catalogs and PDFs vary, with no reusable public bulk licence or common API verified. Request ISBN-level ONIX, CSV or licensed catalog feeds and metadata updates from rights desks. | Manually cite bibliographic facts. Recording a source link or purchase link does not copy a blurb, image or text. Permission for each payload and machine use remains open. [EAEP rights](https://www.eastafricanpublishers.com/rights-catalogue/) · [Longhorn catalog](https://www.longhornpublishers.com/products-services/catalogue/) · [Moran catalog](https://moranpublishers.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/MORAN-PUBLISHERS-2019-CATALOGUE.pdf) |
| Publishers, authors and official bibliographies | African Books Collective identifies Margaret A. Ogola and Focus Books’ *The River and the Source*. Ngũgĩ’s website distinguishes essays and memoirs. The Green Belt Movement lists Maathai’s titles. Paper Lion resolves the Mwenye Hadithi pen name and provides direct evidence of a Kenyan connection; that is not a citizenship claim. | Human-reviewed lookup is feasible. An author’s site may omit older or translated editions. Keep date, URL, field and cited entity. Ask for a data feed if the volume justifies it. | Use bibliographic facts and original editor-written summaries from verified facts. No wholesale prose or cover copying. Request express excerpt and embedding rights and confirm the contracting party controls the relevant edition and translation. [ABC](https://africanbookscollective.com/books/the-river-and-the-source/) · [Ngũgĩ](https://ngugiwathiongo.com/) · [GBM](https://www.greenbeltmovement.org/books/) · [Paper Lion](https://www.paperlionltd.com/clients/bruce-hobson/) |
| National bibliography, universities, literary archives | KNLS has the national bibliography, which is particularly useful for names missing from international aggregators. Nordic Africa Institute lists Kenya-linked works. Pwani and Daystar catalog records help on *Coming to Birth* and *Kidagaa kimemwozea*. Indiana University identifies *Juices* as poems. The Columbia Swahili guide and African Union library provide additional authority leads. | KNLS bibliography PDFs, library OPACs and selected archival catalog pages. Ask about MARC/MARCXML, licensed catalog exports, and harvesting policy per institution. A library holdings record is not proof of current availability. | Cite selected facts and holdings URLs. The availability of a thesis, archive scan or excerpt is not a licence for processing or reuse. Inspect item-level terms and third-party content. [KNLS](https://www.knls.ac.ke/kenya-national-bibliography/) · [Nordic Africa Institute](https://nai.uu.se/download/18.7d64009619390148984e91f9/1734530683565/fiction-kenya-2024-dec.pdf) · [Indiana](https://iucat.iu.edu/iuk/1905138) · [Daystar](https://library.daystar.ac.ke/bib/43380) · [Columbia](https://guides.library.columbia.edu/swahilibib/literature) |
| Project Gutenberg | Useful when an exact work and usable edition are available. Modern Kenyan trade coverage and Kiswahili coverage were not established. Bibliographic records can show language and rights basis. | Catalog feeds and approved mirroring options are preferable to crawling. Check the particular text, its complete licence header and edition information. | No books retrieved. The project assesses U.S. status, and some items are licensed separately. Check Kenya and other processing/storage jurisdictions, translation rights, illustrations, and Gutenberg trademark/boilerplate rules before use. [Permission guidance](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) · [Licence](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) · [Catalog metadata](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/offline_catalogs.html) |
| African Storybook and other explicitly open collections | Promising coverage for children’s texts and local-language versions that international catalogs miss. African Storybook has observed CC BY 4.0 items. StoryWeaver publishes CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements for its story assets. These collections cannot stand in for modern Kenyan adult literature. | Obtain approved exports or bounded access. Record story, version, original author, translator, illustrator, language and the licence displayed on that item. Platform/API and asset-level rights still need checking for any partnership or ingest. | No stories downloaded. CC BY can support reuse of licensed material with attribution and applicable terms. Review rights of linked assets, conditions on derivatives and commercial use, applicable local law, and potential treatment of embeddings before ingestion. [African Storybook example](https://www.africanstorybook.org/newviewer/index.php?bt=3&dual=false&id=49950) · [StoryWeaver attribution](https://storyweaver.org.in/en/attributions) · [CC BY legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) · [CC content-mining guidance](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/Content_mining) |

A public page, an owned copy, a subscription or the ability to open a scan grants no blanket storage or processing permission. Before releasing a text or deriving features, log the exact edition and asset, rights holder, rights basis and evidence, territories, permitted actions, attribution, expiry and removal requirements. Route uncertain cases to a rights reviewer. The prototype used bibliographic API responses and manually inspected publisher, rights-agent and library information. It did not request Google Books or Goodreads data, fetch book text, download covers, or persist provider blurbs. Kenya’s copyright rules can differ from those reflected by a source. Do not extend a rule for one kind of work to translations, artwork, or a specific edition without checking. [Kenya Copyright Act via KECOBO](https://mail.copyright.go.ke/sites/default/files/downloads/CopyrightAct12of2001.pdf)
